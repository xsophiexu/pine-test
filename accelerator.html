For schools & districts

Start strong with the PINE Accelerator

Every new school that joins PINE’s Full Membership begins here. In four live virtual sessions, your leadership team works with PINE coaches and leaves with a plan to launch PINE schoolwide.

What happens each week

Four sessions, one clear plan

This 4-session Accelerator walks your team through the following steps.

  1. 1

    Get to know PINE

    Explore the PINE Platform and its many supports, and get your team organized.

  2. 2

    Look at where you are

    Conduct a whole-organization needs assessment to find your strengths and areas for growth.

  3. 3

    Set your goals

    Set clear, measurable goals for the year ahead.

  4. 4

    Plan the rollout

    Map out the action steps to launch PINE successfully at your school.

Ask about membership

Who participates

Your PINE Leadership Team

Each school or organization chooses a small interdisciplinary team to join the training as its PINE Leadership Team (PLT). Teams often mix administrators, teachers, related service providers and support staff, so the plan reflects the whole school day.

Schools that succeed with PINE usually have a committed core team. That team shares key takeaways with colleagues, keeps everyone engaged and solves problems together.

After the Accelerator

What Full Membership includes all year

  • Online coursework for every role, co-created with autistic and neurodivergent experts
  • Virtual consultation with PINE coaches
  • Professional development activities for staff meetings and working groups
  • Downloadable tools and classroom supports
  • Participation reports to track staff engagement
  • Free entry to the PINE Summit, the annual virtual conference
