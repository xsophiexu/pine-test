Donate on pineprogram.org

Donations are handled securely on PINE’s official site. This concept page doesn’t take payments.

What your gift supports

  • Training for thousands of educators and community leaders each year
  • Courses co-created with neurodivergent experts
  • Consistent, affirming support for children in school and beyond

Ways to give

You can give once, or set up a weekly, monthly, quarterly or annual gift. You can also choose to cover processing fees.

Want to explore a partnership or a larger gift? Email info@pineprogram.org.

Tax information

PINE is a department of New York University, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Donations are tax-deductible to the fullest extent allowed by law. All donations are final and nonrefundable, and no goods or services are provided in exchange.
