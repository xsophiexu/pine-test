About us

The people, purpose and passion behind PINE

PINE helps schools and communities embrace neurodiversity, creating inclusive, accessible and supportive spaces for children wherever they go.

Our roots

PINE is a program of NYU Steinhardt. Our co-founder spent more than a decade building ASD Nest, the largest inclusion program for autistic students in the country. Neurodivergent experts help develop every PINE course.

What guides us

Our values

  • Inclusion

    People of every neurotype feel valued, supported and fully part of their communities.

  • Neurodiversity-affirming practice

    We celebrate neurodivergent perspectives in every learning and community space.

  • Empathy & respect

    We honor each person’s strengths and lead with understanding.

  • Equity & accessibility

    Every learner and community has unique needs that call for responsive support.

  • Systemic change

    We work to take down old barriers so inclusion becomes the foundation.

  • Authentic partnership

    We put neurodivergent and disabled voices at the heart of the work.
